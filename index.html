SpatialFlow

Plan your micro-office or studio layout before you move a single desk.

SpatialFlow is a lightweight layout planner for small teams, co-working studios and creative spaces — drag furniture onto a grid, see it fit, save it, done. This page is a proof-of-concept: everything below runs entirely in your browser.

Try the planner demo See features

Built for spaces too small for enterprise floor-planning tools

Most office-planning software is built for facilities teams managing whole buildings. SpatialFlow is built for the studio down the hall — a handful of desks, a meeting table, maybe a plant that needs a window. Sketch it, share it, ship it.

Why teams use SpatialFlow

A few reasons this fits a micro-office better than a spreadsheet or a whiteboard photo.

🪑

Drag-and-drop layout

Place desks, chairs, meeting tables and plants directly onto a scaled grid of your room.

📐

Fit checking built in

SpatialFlow won't let furniture overlap or hang off the edge of your room — the layout you see is a layout that actually fits.

💾

Your layout, saved locally

Every change saves automatically to your browser. Close the tab, come back tomorrow, pick up where you left off.

⚡

No install, no account

This proof-of-concept is a single page. No sign-up, no backend, nothing to configure.

Try it — plan a small studio

Drag an item from the catalog onto the grid. Drag placed items to move them, use the buttons on a placed item to rotate or remove it. Your layout saves automatically to this browser.
